=====================================
NCollection Invoice Layout & Calculations
=====================================

A money Discount that works both ways with Disc.%, editable Amount and Total incl. Tax (the unit price is solved with Odoo's own
tax engine), centred line headers, Total Discount / Total After Discount above Untaxed Amount, and configurable invoice-level
charges and discounts booked as real invoice lines. Customer invoices and vendor bills; draft invoices only.

Design, formulas, worked examples and what is not built: ``docs/markdown/INVOICE_LAYOUT_AND_CALCULATIONS.md``.
